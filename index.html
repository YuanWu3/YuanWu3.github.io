Yuan Wu

Associate Professor, School of Artificial Intelligence, Jilin University

My research interests include natural language processing, large language models, LLM evaluation, and LLM post-training. I welcome inquiries from undergraduate and graduate students interested in related topics.

About

I am a pre-tenured associate professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Jilin University. I received my Bachelor Degree from Beijing University of Chemical Technology, Master degree from Lanzhou University, advised by Prof. Lian Li, and Ph.D. from Carleton University, advised by Prof. Ahmed El-Roby and Prof. Diana Inkpen.
